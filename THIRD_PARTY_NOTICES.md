## Rust crates in the desktop app

The desktop app compiles in Rust crates from crates.io and Git.  Most are
under MIT or Apache-2.0.  Those under any other licence (BSD-3-Clause,
BSL-1.0, CC0-1.0, CDLA-Permissive-2.0, ISC, MPL-2.0, Unicode-3.0, Zlib, or
MIT or Apache-2.0 combined with another licence) are listed with the licence
and notice files they ship in `THIRD_PARTY_RUST_NOTICES.md`, which installers
include beside this file.  It is generated by
`node scripts/rust-crate-notices.mjs` and checked with `--check`.

The MPL-2.0 crates (`cssparser`, `cssparser-macros`, `dtoa-short`,
`option-ext`, `selectors`) are used unmodified; their source is available from
crates.io and the repositories named in the notices.
